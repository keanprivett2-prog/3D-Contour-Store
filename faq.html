GENERAL

About 3D Contour

What is 3D Contour?

3D Contour is a South African 3D printing business creating personalised cake decorating products, baking tools and custom 3D printed products.

We combine design and 3D printing to turn ideas into physical products.

Where is 3D Contour based?

3D Contour is based in South Africa and supplies customers through our online store.

What can I order from 3D Contour?

Our store includes products such as cake toppers, cookie cutters, stencils, baking tools and other 3D printed creations.

We also offer Custom Printing for customers who need something outside our standard product range.

Can you make something that isn't listed in the shop?

Yes. If you have an idea for something that isn't available in our store, visit our Custom Printing page.

You can provide a design file, image or information about what you would like made, and we'll assess the project.

ORDERING

Placing An Order

How do I place an order?

Browse the store, choose the products you want and add them to your cart.

When you're ready, continue to checkout, enter your details, choose your delivery method and complete your payment.

Can I order more than one of the same product?

Yes. You can increase the quantity of a product directly from your cart or when ordering a custom print.

Larger quantities may require additional production time.

Can I order a personalised product?

Many of our products can be personalised. Product-specific options will be displayed on the relevant product page.

If you need something more specific, you can submit it through our Custom Printing service.

Will I receive an order number?

Yes. Once your order has been successfully processed, you will receive an order number on the order confirmation page.

CUSTOM PRINTING

Your Design, Your Print

I already have an STL file. Can you print it?

Yes. You can upload your STL through our Custom Printing page.

The system can analyse the STL and provide an estimated printing cost based on the available options.

I don't have an STL file. Can you still help?

Yes. You don't necessarily need to start with an STL file.

You can provide an image, reference or description of what you would like and we'll assess what is required to turn the idea into a printable design.

Can I upload an image instead of an STL?

Yes. Our Custom Printing page allows you to upload an image if you don't have an STL.

An image is used as a reference for the project and does not automatically provide the same pricing information as a print-ready STL.

Can you design something for me?

We can assess custom design requests. Send us your idea, reference image, dimensions and any other information you have available.

We'll let you know what is possible and what information is required before the project proceeds.

Can you print something I found online?

If you have a printable model that you are legally entitled to use, you can send it to us for consideration.

Please make sure that the designer's licensing terms allow the model to be printed commercially before submitting it for an order.

Can you print large quantities?

Yes. We can consider larger quantities, subject to the size and complexity of the product and our available production capacity.

Large orders may require additional production time.

MATERIALS & COLOUR

Choosing Your Filament

What materials do you print with?

Our Custom Printing system currently provides PLA, PETG and TPU options.

The best material depends on what you're making and how the finished product will be used.

What is PLA?

PLA is a commonly used 3D printing material that is well suited to many decorative and general-purpose prints.

It is particularly popular for products where appearance and ease of printing are important.

What is PETG?

PETG is another commonly used 3D printing material. It can be a useful choice when a project requires characteristics different from standard PLA.

If you're unsure which material is appropriate, contact us before ordering.

What is TPU?

TPU is a flexible 3D printing material. It can be useful for projects where some flexibility is required.

Can I choose my filament colour?

Colour availability depends on the product and the filament we have available at the time of production.

If you require a particular colour, contact us before placing your order so we can confirm availability.

PAINTING & FINISHING

Want To Paint Your Print?

Why might I choose white filament?

White filament can be a great choice if you plan to paint your finished print yourself.

It provides a clean, neutral starting point and gives you the freedom to spray paint the finished item in the colour you want.

This can be especially useful when you need a specific colour that isn't available in our standard filament selection.

Can I spray paint a 3D printed item?

Yes. Many 3D printed items can be painted after printing, provided the appropriate preparation and paint are used.

The final result will depend on the material, surface preparation, paint and application method.

Do I need to use primer before spray painting?

For best results, we recommend applying a suitable primer before spray painting a 3D printed item.

Primer helps prepare the surface for the paint and can improve the final appearance and adhesion.

If the spray paint you're using already contains a suitable primer, a separate primer may not be necessary. Always follow the instructions provided by the paint manufacturer.

PRICING

Understanding Your Price

How much does a custom 3D print cost?

Custom printing prices depend on the requirements of the print, including the model, material and quantity.

When an STL is uploaded through our Custom Printing page, the system can provide an estimated printing cost.

Why can the price of a custom print vary?

Different models use different amounts of material and can require different amounts of machine time.

Material selection and quantity can also affect the total cost.

Is the custom-print price an estimate?

The price calculated from an uploaded STL is an estimate based on the information available to the system.

If a project requires additional design work or has requirements that cannot be determined automatically, further assessment may be required.

Is there a minimum custom-print order?

Yes. Our Custom Printing service currently has a minimum order value of R35.

PRODUCTION

From Order To Print

How long does an order take?

Production time depends on the products ordered, the quantity, the complexity of the print and our current production schedule.

Larger orders may require additional production time.

Can I request my order by a specific date?

We are working towards allowing customers to select their required date during checkout.

Production time will need to be taken into account, particularly for larger quantities.

If you have an important deadline, please contact us before placing the order so we can discuss your requirements.

Can I place an urgent order?

Urgent orders depend on the size of the order, the required date, our current production schedule and material availability.

Please contact us before ordering if you have a particularly tight deadline.

DELIVERY & COLLECTION

Getting Your Order

Do you offer collection?

Yes. Collection is available as a delivery option during checkout.

Collection currently has no delivery fee.

Do you offer PUDO delivery?

Yes. We currently offer PUDO delivery through two options:

  • Locker to Locker
  • Locker to Address
How much is PUDO Locker to Locker delivery?

PUDO Locker to Locker delivery is currently R80 .

How much is PUDO Locker to Address delivery?

When does the courier collect my order?

Your order needs to be completed and ready for dispatch before it can be handed over for delivery.
